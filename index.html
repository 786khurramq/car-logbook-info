Car Logbook

An Android app for keeping vehicle records, costs, documents and reminders.

Privacy policy

Your records are stored on your phone. The developer does not receive or have access to them.

If you connect a Google account, the app uses Google Drive access only to create, read and update its own backup file in your Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. This data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

You can remove the connection in the app's settings or your Google account, and delete the backup file from your Drive.
